FAQ

Common Questions

I have no IT background. Can I still join?
Yes. CCNA, MikroTik, and Linux courses all start from absolute zero. The free demo class will help you and the trainer confirm the right starting point for you.
Are classes online or physical?
Both. Most courses run onsite in Lahore with full lab access, and several are also offered as live online batches. Online students get remote lab access for practice.
Will I get a certificate?
Yes — every student receives a verified completion certificate from SoftSkill IT. We also prepare you for the official vendor exams (Cisco, MikroTik, Fortinet, AWS), which are separate international certifications.
Do you help with jobs?
Every course includes CV building, LinkedIn profile review, and mock technical interviews. We also refer strong students to openings in our industry network. We prepare you thoroughly, though final hiring always depends on the employer.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. Every course has an installment plan — typically a registration amount to reserve your seat, with the remainder split across the course duration. Details are shared during admission.
What are the batch timings?
Morning, evening, and weekend batches run for most courses, designed around students and working professionals. New batches start every month; seats are capped at 8–10 students.
